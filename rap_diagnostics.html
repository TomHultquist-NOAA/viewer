Read-only checks for the hourly RAP F000 analyses used by the Analysis panel. The two catalogs are queried over HTTP as the browser sees them; processing-server local stores and unpublished data cannot be inspected from here.

Select Check RAP products.

RAP is a model analysis. The Analysis panel chooses data only when its selected field is present at the exact verifying UTC hour. It cannot display a RAP product that was not converted and published, even when forecast-model data for the cycle are complete.
